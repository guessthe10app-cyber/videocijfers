Privacy Policy

Videocijfers connects only to social media accounts owned by the person who runs the tool.

It reads the public details of that person's own videos (title, publication date, thumbnail, duration) and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares and similar figures). It also reads the account's follower count.

This data is stored privately by the owner and is used only to track how their own videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data about other users is collected.

The owner can disconnect an account at any time in the settings of TikTok or Instagram. Stored figures are deleted when the owner removes them.
